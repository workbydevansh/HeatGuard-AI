What The System Does

HeatGuard AI combines time-aware feature engineering, location history, lag and rolling climate signals, tree-based forecasting, explainability, risk thresholds, and advisory generation into a single challenge-ready workflow.

1

Forecast: predicts wet-bulb temperature for target days 1 through 10.

2

Classify: maps WBT output into low, moderate, high, and extreme risk bands.

3

Act: produces practical heat action guidance for multiple public-health audiences.
